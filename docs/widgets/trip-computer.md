# TripComputer

!!! info "Planned"
    `TripComputer` is specified but not written yet: this page describes what it will show.

![Preview of TripComputer, dark theme](../img/widgets/trip-computer-dark.png#only-dark)

*Preview: two anywidget-instruments [`SevenSegment`](https://s-celles.github.io/anywidget-instruments/widgets/seven-segment/) displays, as the [cluster preview](../examples.md) draws it — not yet
`TripComputer` itself. Captured from the running preview, in the theme of this page.*

The figures of a trip: instant and average consumption, fuel used, distance, elapsed
time, range. Each figure says what it rests on — an average over 0.1 km is noise and is
not shown — and a consumption at a standstill is given per hour, not per 100 km.
